Ops Notes

How this MVP fits your launch plan

What works now

This prototype is immediately playable in a browser, stores weekly scores locally, and gives you an exportable view of the current week's winners and giveaway pool.

What to connect next

Replace local storage with a small backend so scores sync across all visitors, then embed the game into Shopify or wrap it into a mobile app shell later.

Why no screenshots

Screenshot submissions create manual work and are easier to fake. A real score form gives you ranking, contact details, and giveaway eligibility in one place.
